# Code of Conduct

Okoscope adopts and follows the
[CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).
It applies to all project spaces and activities, including repositories, issue
trackers, reviews, meetings, chat, events, and private communication made in the
context of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a private
contact method listed on the GitHub profiles in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

A maintainer who is named in or has a conflict of interest concerning a report
must recuse themselves. The remaining non-conflicted maintainers will promptly
acknowledge the report, protect the reporter's privacy as far as possible,
investigate it fairly, and apply proportionate corrective action under the CNCF
Code of Conduct. If no non-conflicted maintainer is available, the reporter may
escalate to the CNCF conduct contacts listed in the CNCF Code of Conduct.

Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report or assists an
investigation is prohibited.
